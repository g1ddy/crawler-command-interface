# Floor 3 Inventory, Equipment, Loot, and Award Research Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This report establishes the Stage 1 canon/deep-research evidence pass for *Dungeon Crawler Carl, Book 2: Carl's Doomsday Scenario* (Floor 3: The Over City), focusing on inventory items, equipment, loot acquisitions, item creation/transformation/destruction, and award state transitions.

The research scope covers Floor 3 chronological events while preserving continuity from Floor 2 and forward into Floor 4 where evidence explicitly establishes persistence. The report operates under strict domain boundaries, cleanly separating:
1. **Acquisition vs. Possession**: Item possession does not imply Floor 3 acquisition unless explicitly evidenced.
2. **Equipment vs. Possession**: Possessing an item does not imply it is equipped, worn, attached, or wielded.
3. **Award Lifecycle Chains**: Award causation (achievement/trigger) is distinguished from grant, delivery, receipt, opening, content extraction, and subsequent use or transfer.

Key findings of this research pass include:
- **Hobby Potions**: Carl consumes an Earth Hobby Potion yielding the Cesta Punta skill, a Home Depot cactus, and Louis L'Amour paperback books. Donut consumes an Earth Hobby Potion yielding the Scutelliphily skill (whose mechanics remain unrevealed in research), a large cat tree, and a scented candle.
- **Loot & Acquisitions**: Donut loots the Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak from Grace Bautista's corpse. Carl loots Big Top Tickets from dead clown sentries. Carl ingests parasitic worms in an ice cream cone from Apollon the Mighty to establish a telepathic link with Ringmaster Grimaldi. Carl uses a Fireball or Custard lottery ticket during the fight with Heather the Bear, yielding a custard result that heals Heather and removes parasite control.
- **Equipment & Upgrades**: Carl receives a Scroll of Upgrade from his Platinum Quest Box and applies it to his Enchanted BigBoi Boxers, granting the Freeballing benefit (increasing leg/foot damage while preventing lower-body armor stacking). Carl consumes the Tome of Wisp Armor, gaining immunity to mental overrides. Donut receives Enchanted Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk from an upgraded Quest Box and transfers them to Mongo, who equips them. Donut unequips the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore during wild magic precursor pulses, triggering its intrinsic Fleeting property and permanently destroying it.
- **Explosives & Containment**: Carl acquires a Pharmaceutical Starter Kit, Blitz Sticks, Hoblobbers, and Hobgoblin Dynamite/remote detonators (one of which is physically transferred to Katia Grim). Carl encapsulates an overloaded Soul Crystal inside a Sheol Glass Reaper Case, creating Carl's Doomsday Scenario.
- **Boxes & Awards**: Reaching 1 quadrillion views awards Carl and Donut Fan Boxes locked for 30 hours. Unlocking yields Carl's Xistera and Donut's framed photograph of Beatrice. The Hadji and Bandit achievements trigger an AI upgrade of the party's Platinum Quest Boxes to Celestial Quest Boxes (yielding a reported total of 83 Celestial-quality boxes across affected crawlers), but Borant exercises an administrative veto that prevents actual delivery of the Celestial boxes.

This report is structured to serve directly as the source for Stage 2 YAML extraction under the `crawler-research/v1` schema without requiring further evidence discovery.

## 4. Floor 3 Chronology

The chronological sequence of inventory, equipment, loot, and award events on Floor 3 follows the progression below:

1. **Floor Entry & Safe Room Setup (Ch. 1–2)**:
   - Carl and Donut enter Floor 3 (Over City).
   - In the Belly-Rubbed Pug Safe Room, Carl and Donut consume their Earth Hobby Potions.
   - Carl receives Cesta Punta skill, cactus, and Louis L'Amour paperbacks.
   - Donut receives Scutelliphily skill, cat tree, and scented candle.
2. **First Encounter & Grace Bautista Loot (Ch. 4–5)**:
   - Fight with former circus mobs / Grace Bautista.
   - Donut loots the Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak from Grace's corpse.
3. **View Milestone & Fan Box Delivery (Ch. 8)**:
   - Party hits 1 quadrillion view milestone.
   - Fan Boxes awarded with a 30-hour temporal lock.
   - Upon lock expiry, Carl extracts his Xistera and Donut extracts the framed picture of Beatrice.
4. **Quest Box Opening & Upgrades (Ch. 11–12)**:
   - Quest completion rewards distributed.
   - Carl opens Platinum Quest Box: receives 5,000 gold and Scroll of Upgrade.
   - Carl applies Scroll of Upgrade to Enchanted BigBoi Boxers (gaining Freeballing).
   - Carl opens Lucky Bastard Box: receives 100,000-gold casino chip.
   - Donut opens upgraded Quest Box: receives 5,000 gold, Acute Ears skill potion, and Enchanted Fang Caps.
   - Donut transfers Enchanted Fang Caps to Mongo; Mongo equips them.
5. **Mid-Floor Consumables & Parasites (Ch. 15–18)**:
   - Carl consumes Tome of Wisp Armor, obtaining mental override immunity.
   - Carl ingests ice cream cone with parasitic worms from Apollon the Mighty, establishing a telepathic link with Grimaldi.
   - Battle with Heather the Bear: Carl uses Fireball/Custard ticket, resulting in custard healing and parasite purge.
   - Carl loots Big Top Tickets from clown sentries.
6. **Magistrate's Office & Wild Magic Pulses (Ch. 21–22)**:
   - Infiltration of Miss Quill's / Magistrate's office.
   - Wild magic precursor pulses activate.
   - Auto-drinking of health and mana potions from digital inventory.
   - Donut unequips the Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore; item's Fleeting property destroys the crown permanently.
7. **Explosives & Doomsday Assembly (Ch. 24–25)**:
   - Carl transfers a hobgoblin remote detonator to Katia Grim.
   - Carl encapsulates an overloaded Soul Crystal into a Sheol Glass Reaper Case, creating Carl's Doomsday Scenario.
8. **Floor Exit & Award Veto (Ch. 25–26 / Epilogue)**:
   - Party escapes via Red Line Car 20 stairwell to Floor 4.
   - Hadji and Bandit achievements trigger reward calculation: AI upgrades Platinum Quest Boxes to 83 Celestial Quest Boxes across top crawlers.
   - Borant issues an administrative veto blocking physical delivery of the Celestial Quest Boxes.
