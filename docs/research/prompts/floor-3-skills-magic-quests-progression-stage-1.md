# Stage 1 Research — Floor 3 Skills, Magic, Quests, and Progression Capabilities

## Purpose

Produce the evidence-rich research report used by Stage 2 for Issue #184. Research Book 2 / Floor 3 (The Over City), focusing on skills, magic, explicit quests, and persistent progression capabilities.

This is canon research, not CCI modeling. Do not produce YAML/JSON, runtime events, UI behavior, or implementation recommendations.

## Research scope

Investigate:

- Carl's Floor 3 race/class progression and persistent capability changes, especially Primal and Compensated Anarchist.
- New or materially changed skills and skill progression, including explicit skill mutations, training limits, and practical capability changes.
- New or materially changed magic/spells for Carl or Donut, including explicit effects and persistent upgrades.
- Explicit Floor 3 quests and quest progression, including activation, completion, resolution path, and supported rewards.
- Progression capabilities that become mechanically meaningful on Floor 3, including pet/familiar progression only where it directly establishes a broader progression capability.
- Chronology needed to understand how these capabilities and quests relate to one another.

Use earlier/later material only when it establishes continuity or clarifies a Floor 3 fact. Preserve the actual chronology of the supporting evidence.

## Evidence rules

Use the best accessible web evidence. The published book may not be directly accessible.

Do not claim to have inspected the physical book when the evidence is a secondary web source. A secondary source can directly state a proposition; that does not make the source primary.

For each substantive claim, record:

- exact web source/page;
- URL;
- source type/trust;
- chapter/section/other locator when available;
- what the source directly states;
- important uncertainty or qualification;
- whether the conclusion is direct, corroborated, inferred, or unknown.

Do not require multiple sources when one credible source is the best accessible evidence. Do not treat repeated copies of the same summary as independent corroboration.

Source trust and claim confidence are separate. A corroborating source can support a claim strongly enough for the claim to be confirmed.

Preserve unknowns. Do not invent exact wording, levels, quantities, mechanics, chronology, rewards, or System rules.

Do not generalize a character-specific observation into a universal System rule unless the evidence establishes the broader rule.

## Required research areas

### Skills

Research concrete Floor 3 skill changes and progression, including:

- Primal-related skill capability/training changes.
- Iron Punch / Breadbasket mutation and its trigger/effect.
- Trap detection/disarm or trap-engineering capability where the evidence establishes a meaningful Floor 3 progression change.
- Any other newly acquired or materially upgraded skill that is explicitly supported.

### Magic

Research concrete spell/capability changes, including:

- Wisp Armor and each independently established effect.
- Donut's Magic Missile progression/upgrades.
- Shield/Forcewall or other significant defensive magic.
- Other Floor 3 spell changes only where evidence supports them.

Keep spell capability separate from the quest in which it is used.

### Quests

Research explicit Floor 3 quests, including:

- The Show Must Go On.
- HOA / Neighborhood Cleansing.
- Retrieve Maestro's Instruments and the Grimaldi sequence.
- Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens / CockBlock.
- Fools Who Broke the Glass / Bandit outcome.

For each quest, distinguish activation, progression, completion/resolution, and reward consequences when evidence supports them.

### Progression

Research:

- Primal race selection and its persistent consequences.
- Compensated Anarchist class consequences.
- Pet/familiar progression only where it establishes a relevant Floor 3 capability or interface/system progression.
- The chronology connecting these progression changes to Floor 3 events.

## Atomicity

Separate independently useful propositions.

For example, treat these separately:

- Wisp Armor's magic-damage mitigation.
- Wisp Armor's mind-control/psionic immunity.
- The Show Must Go On resolution.
- The achievement/reward consequence of that resolution.

Likewise, distinguish:

- Fools Who Broke the Glass resolution.
- Survival of the affected crawlers.
- Bandit achievement trigger.
- Reward-tier consequence.

The figure 83 must remain a population/event fact, not a personal Carl inventory quantity.
