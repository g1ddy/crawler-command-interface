# Stage 1 Research — Dungeon Crawler Carl Book 2, Floor 3
## Achievements, Entitlements, Crafting, Inventory, Equipment, Loot, and Award State

### Role and goal
Research Book 2, *Carl's Doomsday Scenario*, focusing on Floor 3 / The Over City. Produce a human-readable, evidence-rich report for later Stage 2 extraction. You are a canon researcher, not a CCI designer. Do not create YAML/JSON, runtime models, application event types, IDs, UI behavior, or implementation recommendations.

### Scope
Research:
- achievements, triggers, recipients, notifications, rewards, reward modifications, and causal links;
- entitlements and unlocks, including Desperado Club access, passes, titles, permissions, class benefits, conditions, benefits, restrictions, and duration;
- crafting/workstations, explicit crafting actions, improvised construction, recipes/materials if stated, and relevant class abilities;
- inventory/equipment actions: acquisition, transfer, equip/unequip, storage, reading, use, consumption, loss, destruction, and item properties;
- loot and rewards: quest boxes, box upgrades, contents, recipients, award timing, opening/claiming, withholding, vetoes, and partial outcomes;
- chronology and causal relationships among these events.

Use earlier/later book material only when it clarifies continuity, terminology, or a Floor 3 event. Identify the actual chronology and source book. Do not present Floor 4 events as Floor 3 events.

### Evidence and source policy
Use the best accessible evidence. Prefer primary book text or official sources when available, but credible secondary sources are valid and expected evidence when primary text cannot be found online. Relevant sources may include detailed fan wikis, chapter summaries, fan compendia, interviews, articles, and community discussions.

A clear claim from a credible secondary source may be reported as confirmed for this research workflow. Do not mark it unknown solely because the book passage is inaccessible. However:
- Identify source type and limitations honestly.
- Do not imply you inspected the book if you did not.
- A secondary source's direct statement is direct evidence of what that source reports, not direct inspection of the novel.
- Identify a quotation reproduced by a secondary source as such unless you checked the book text.
- Do not count pages repeating the same underlying account as independent corroboration.
- Keep source statements separate from your interpretations.
- Preserve disagreement, ambiguity, speculation, and gaps.
- Absence from a source is not evidence that something did not happen.
- One credible, specific source can be sufficient; do not require multiple sources solely for confirmation.

For each material finding record source ID, exact title/site, exact URL when available, source type, locator when supplied, short quotation or clearly marked paraphrase, evidence label, and relevant limitations. Never invent a URL, quotation, locator, quantity, item property, mechanic, or causal relationship.

### Evidence labels
- DIRECT — The source itself directly states the proposition. Identify whether it is primary or secondary; DIRECT does not imply book-text verification.
- CORROBORATED — Materially independent sources reinforce the proposition.
- INFERRED — Reasoned interpretation not explicitly established by the available evidence.
- UNKNOWN — Available evidence does not establish the requested detail.
- DISPUTED — Credible sources conflict or support materially different interpretations.

### Research method
Extract narrow useful propositions. Separate action, outcome, later consequence, recipient, affected population, and interpretation. Preserve chronology only as precisely as evidence allows. Distinguish a one-time example from a general system rule. Keep canon terminology. Do not infer universal mechanics from a single example.

### Focus questions

#### Achievements and rewards
- What achievements are named, what triggers them, and who receives them?
- What does each notification state?
- What reward is granted, upgraded, unlocked, or announced?
- Is causation explicit or inferred?
- Are award, delivery, opening, claiming, and item acquisition distinct?

#### The Fools Who Broke the Glass / Bandit!
- Quest conditions, affected area, participants/survivors, original reward and quantities.
- Bandit! trigger, recipients, exact reward change, and box count.
- What happens to Quan Ch's box and resulting item?
- What happens to remaining boxes after Borant's intervention?
- Which totals are stated versus calculated?
- Does evidence establish a general veto rule or only this outcome?

#### Entitlements and access
- Desperado Club access mechanism and how Carl/Donut obtain it.
- Pass/tattoo, Asshole Box, boss-kill, or other conditions as explicitly supported.
- Titles, class benefits, permissions, recipients, timing, benefits, restrictions, and duration.
- Club sub-areas, Privacy Bubbles, travel/exit restrictions, pocket-dimension descriptions, pet restrictions.
- Separate one character's access route from universal requirements.

#### Crafting and workstations
- Workbench/station identity, origin, properties, and use.
- What action is performed and what results?
- Is it explicitly crafting or an improvised construction/containment action?
- Are recipe, materials, station requirement, timer, or system notification stated?
- What class/skill is relevant and what effect is established?
- Do not infer a formal crafting system from an improvised action.

#### Inventory, equipment, loot
Investigate relevant items where supported, including Wisp Armor Spell Book, Heart of a Mold Lion, “Fireball or Custard?” Lottery Ticket, Carl's Doomsday Scenario / Sheol case, quest boxes, Quan Ch's reward, and other material Floor 3 items.
For each, distinguish exact supported name, acquisition, transfer, possession, storage, equip/unequip, reading, use, consumption, effect, loss, and later state. Do not invent persistence, removability, durability, degradation, or quantity.

#### Degradation and item properties
What does evidence say about system inventory, backpacks, safe rooms, explosives, degradation, instability, and preservation? Is it an explicit general rule, a character belief, or one observed example? Identify which item/circumstances are covered. Keep secondary-source-derived mechanics qualified.

### Quantities and partial outcomes
Keep participants, survivors, achievement recipients, reward recipients, boxes awarded/upgraded/opened/claimed/withheld, and resulting items distinct. Tie every count to its population and outcome. Show arithmetic for derived totals. Preserve unresolved timing or conflicting counts rather than forcing reconciliation.

### Output structure
1. Executive Summary
2. Scope and Source Method
3. Chronological Findings
4. Achievements and Causal Rewards
5. Entitlements and System Unlocks
6. Crafting and Workstation Evidence
7. Inventory, Equipment, and Loot
8. Award Quantities and Partial Outcomes
9. Contradictions, Ambiguities, and Unknowns
10. Targeted Verification / Follow-up Queue
11. Source Register

Every material finding must have a source reference, source type, evidence label, locator if available, and uncertainty. The source register must resolve references and preserve exact URLs when available. The follow-up queue should target consequential ambiguity, conflict, uncertain names/counts, unsupported causation, or places where better evidence would materially help; do not require primary verification for every secondary-source finding.

### Output constraints
Markdown only. No YAML/JSON or CCI modeling. Do not invent IDs, quantities, recipes, properties, mechanics, persistence, or causal links. Preserve unknowns. Aim for a traceable report that captures the best available evidence and can be extracted without losing provenance or uncertainty.
