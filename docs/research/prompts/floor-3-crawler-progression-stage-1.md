# Stage 1 Research — Floor 3 Crawler Progression, Statistics, and Conditions

## Purpose

Produce an evidence-rich Markdown research report for Issue #182, “Floor 3 research: crawler progression, stats, and condition anchors.” Research Book 2 / Floor 3 (The Over City), focusing on Carl's progression, explicitly reported statistics/resources, conditions/effects, and chronology.

This is canon research, not CCI modeling. Do not produce YAML/JSON, runtime events, UI behavior, or implementation recommendations.

## Research scope

Investigate:
- Carl's Floor 3 race/class selection and consequential persistent progression.
- Level and progression anchors, including only source-supported levels and chapter/section boundaries.
- Attribute values, unallocated points, XP/max XP, and health/mana/stamina only when explicitly reported.
- Injuries, unconsciousness, temporary conditions, debuffs, beneficial/harmful effects, and recovery boundaries.
- Whether sources explicitly establish an empty effect list or instead leave effect state unreported.
- Chronology and countdown references needed to place claims accurately.
- Continuity from Floors 1–2 only where evidence establishes persistence or clarifies a Floor 3 fact.

## Evidence and source rules

Use the best accessible web evidence. The published novel may not be directly accessible online.

For each substantive claim, record the exact source/page and URL, source type/trust, locator where available, what the source directly states, and relevant uncertainty or qualification. Keep source trust separate from claim confidence.

The expected evidence base is accessible online research; do not assume access to the physical novel. Do not ask the user to confirm claims against the book text, and do not treat primary-text access as a prerequisite for completing the research. A secondary source can directly state a proposition without being primary-text verification. Do not count pages that repeat the same underlying account as independent corroboration.

Use these confidence definitions consistently:
- `confirmed`: available evidence establishes the proposition strongly enough for this workflow. Multiple credible, materially independent online sources may collectively establish a claim as confirmed; exceptionally strong, specific evidence from one credible source can also qualify. No material conflict remains unresolved. This does not mean the novel was inspected.
- `corroborated`: credible evidence supports the proposition, but the evidence is narrower, less specific, less independent, or otherwise not strong enough for confirmed.
- `candidate`: plausible but weak, incomplete, indirect, or insufficiently traceable support.
- `disputed`: credible sources materially conflict or significant ambiguity remains unresolved.

Source trust and claim confidence are independent. Keep secondary sources classified as secondary even when confidence is confirmed. Count independent evidence chains, not URLs.

Never invent quotations, locators, values, mechanics, durations, or causal relationships. Absence from a source is not evidence that something did not happen. Preserve unknowns and contradictions.

## Required safeguards

- Do not infer an earlier maximum level from a later level snapshot. A level reported at Floor 3 entry does not establish the highest level achieved on Floors 1–2.
- Do not calculate attributes from race/class modifiers or assumed baselines unless the source explicitly reports the resulting values.
- Do not treat unreported health, mana, stamina, XP, max values, or stat points as zero.
- Do not treat an omitted or incomplete effects list as an explicit report of no active effects.
- Keep observed readings separate from inferred or causally derived values.
- Preserve the floor/timer reference for countdowns. Do not convert or compare countdowns across different floors or anchors unless the evidence explicitly establishes the relationship.
- Keep start, duration, and recovery/end boundaries distinct; do not infer a precise end time from approximate narrative language.
- Do not generalize a character-specific observation into a universal System rule without evidence.
- Do not promote a secondary-source report to primary-text verification.
- Do not create parallel timelines or recommend CCI event types.
