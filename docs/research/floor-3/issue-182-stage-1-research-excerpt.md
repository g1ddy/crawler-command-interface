# Floor 3 Crawler Progression, Statistics, and Conditions — Stage 1 Research Excerpt

## Status and scope

This is a deliberately limited excerpt of Stage 1 research for Issue #182, “Floor 3 research: crawler progression, stats, and condition anchors.” It is not the complete research report and does not independently verify every proposition that may appear in a Stage 2 extraction. Stage 2 must preserve this boundary: extract only claims supported by the supplied excerpt and its cited evidence; claims found elsewhere during separate research must not be presented as though this excerpt confirmed them.

The accessible evidence summarized here is secondary web material. The underlying novel has not been directly inspected for this excerpt. “Confirmed” in a downstream research ledger can mean sufficiently established for this workflow; it must not imply primary-text verification.

## Progression anchors

- Secondary research reports that Carl selects the Primal race during Floor 3 progression.
- The research describes Primal as imposing a persistent one-point reduction to each of the five base attributes.
- The research describes Primal as allowing skills to be trained up to Level 20, beyond the standard Level 15 training ceiling.
- Carl selects the Compensated Anarchist class. Reported class modifiers include a -25% bladed-weapon damage penalty and a +25% mana-cost penalty for attack spells.
- These are reported race/class properties. They do not, by themselves, establish Carl's resulting attribute values, health/mana/stamina values, or the exact sequence/timing of every level transition.

## Reported level and attribute information

- Secondary research reports Carl entering the Floor 3 class-selection process at Level 13 with 36 unallocated stat points.
- A secondary player-stats page reports unenhanced pre-selection attributes of Strength 6, Intelligence 3, Constitution 5, Dexterity 5, and Charisma 4.
- The same research reports 23 points allocated to meet class prerequisites (17 Charisma and 6 Dexterity), with the remaining 13 allocated to Strength (5), Intelligence (2), and Constitution (6).
- A secondary player-stats page reports finalized unenhanced attributes after race/class selection of Strength 10, Intelligence 5, Constitution 10, Dexterity 10, and Charisma 25.
- Secondary research reports Carl concluding Floor 3 at Level 27.
- These reported snapshots do not establish exact intermediate level-transition times, XP/max XP values, or exact maximum health, mana, or stamina. Do not interpolate those values.

## Condition and effect anchors

- A secondary page for “Sore as Shit” describes a debuff involving intense pain and muscle soreness for a minimum of 60 minutes after significant kinetic trauma.
- A separate research report describes Carl being unconscious for fifteen hours beginning at a Floor 3 countdown reference. The exact timer anchor and any conversion should be retained as stated in the complete source report; do not merge it with another floor's countdown.
- The evidence summarized in this excerpt does not establish an exhaustive active-effects list at any arbitrary timeline point. Unless a source explicitly reports that there are no active effects, effect state must remain unknown/not established rather than being interpreted as an empty list.
