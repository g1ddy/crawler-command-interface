# Floor 3 Skills, Magic, Quests, and Progression — Stage 1 Research Excerpt

## Scope

This excerpt captures the research findings relevant to Issue #184: Floor 3 / Book 2 skills, magic, explicit quests, and persistent progression capabilities.

The evidence is web-accessible secondary evidence. The underlying novel is not being treated as directly inspected. Source trust and claim confidence are separate: corroborating sources may establish claims strongly enough to be treated as confirmed for CCI authoring.

## Progression

### Primal

- Carl selects the Primal race during the Floor 3 progression/staging sequence.
- The Primal selection is persistent and establishes ongoing racial modifiers/capabilities.
- The Primal capability removes the standard Level 15 skill-training ceiling and allows skills to reach Level 20, with exponential scaling described by the source.
- The Primal race imposes a permanent one-point reduction to each of the five base attributes.
- Exact System Prompt wording for the selection remains unresolved.

### Compensated Anarchist

The researched Floor 3 material identifies persistent class modifiers including:

- -25% bladed-weapon damage.
- +25% mana cost for attack spells.
- +5% bomb/trap training.

These are class-level persistent modifiers, not temporary event effects.

## Skills

### Iron Punch / Breadbasket

Carl's Iron Punch mutates into Breadbasket at Level 10. The researched source describes a persistent 10% probability of inflicting a severe stun on a solid strike.

The research does not establish whether Level 10 is a universal Pugilism mutation threshold.

### Trap capability

The Floor 3 research identifies trap detection/disarm / trap-engineering capability as a meaningful progression area, including practical use during the HOA/neighborhood sequence. Exact skill naming should remain source-scoped where the evidence does not establish the exact System label.

## Magic

### Wisp Armor

Two independently useful effects are established:

1. Wisp Armor negates 75% of incoming magic damage.
2. Wisp Armor provides temporary absolute immunity to mind-control/psionic effects.

Carl later uses Wisp Armor as part of the diplomatic resolution of The Show Must Go On. The capability and the quest resolution are separate facts.

### Donut magic progression

The research identifies a Floor 3 Magic Missile upgrade involving additional projectiles/targeting.

The research also identifies Shield/Forcewall as a significant defensive capability acquired by Donut.

Exact System wording should remain unresolved unless directly supported by the accessible evidence.

## Quests

### The Show Must Go On

The quest is resolved diplomatically when Carl uses Wisp Armor to broker a telepathic truce with the Ringmaster.

The diplomatic resolution triggers the Hadji Achievement and elevates the reward to a Platinum Quest Box.

### HOA / Neighborhood Cleansing

Carl and Donut undertake the HOA / Neighborhood Cleansing quest. The researched progression involves activating the quest, clearing houses/the neighborhood, and completing the quest through the HOA leadership/boss resolution.

Exact title wording and unsupported reward details should remain source-scoped.

### Retrieve Maestro's Instruments

Maestro gives Carl and Donut a quest to retrieve his instruments from the Grimaldi brothers. Returning the instruments completes the quest and produces supported quest rewards.

Exact reward details should remain unresolved where the accessible evidence does not establish them.

### Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens / CockBlock

Carl's assassination of Miss Quill causes the quest/reward situation to fail in a way that triggers the CockBlock achievement and transfers the relevant reward outcome to Carl.

The quest failure and achievement/reward consequence should remain distinct claims.

### Fools Who Broke the Glass / Bandit

Carl resolves Fools Who Broke the Glass by sealing an overloading Soul Crystal in a Sheol Glass Reaper Case. The intervention saves 83 nearby crawlers / prevents the intended mass-casualty outcome.

The unexpected survival triggers the Bandit achievement, which affects the reward tier.

The 83-person figure describes the affected crawler population. It is not Carl's personal inventory quantity.

## Chronology

The researched progression is broadly:

Primal selection → Over City / HOA progression → Mongo/Pet progression becomes mechanically relevant → Maestro quest → Grimaldi/instrument sequence → Floor 4 transition.

This chronology should not be treated as an exact timestamped sequence where the sources do not provide that precision.
