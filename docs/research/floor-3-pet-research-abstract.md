# Floor 3 Pet Research Abstract

## Scope

This research extract captures source-backed evidence relevant to the Floor 3 Pet domain, with emphasis on Mongo, pet progression, condition and recovery, equipment, containment, deployment, and pet-related System behavior.

The research target is Floor 3, but individual evidence retains its own chronology. Earlier-book evidence is included where it establishes the provenance or continuity of a Floor 3 pet state.

## Findings

- Mongo is a dungeon-generated pet-class Mongoliensis hatched from an egg acquired on Floor 2.
- Mongo reaches multiple documented levels during the researched Floor 3 chronology; the available secondary evidence identifies Levels 6, 10, and 13, while exact encounter/timestamp precision remains unresolved.
- Research sources describe substantial physical growth associated with Mongo's progression, but the extract deliberately preserves this as an observed progression pattern rather than asserting a universal leveling mechanic.
- Mongo is critically wounded while defending Princess Donut from Level-8 Street Urchin mobs. The researched material establishes the injury and recovery sequence without supplying exact damage values or all status effects.
- A standard heal scroll does not resolve Mongo's injuries; the research describes a specific recovery compound involving a healing potion, cinnamon stick, and thistle rot.
- Carl installs the Enchanted Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk on Mongo, and the researched material describes an electrical effect associated with Mongo's bite.
- The party acquires a Magical Pet Carrier on Floor 3. Available evidence describes teleportation into containment and a rest/recovery state, while some broader claims about inventory restrictions require additional verification before being treated as reusable System rules.
- Mongo's resistance to forced carrier teleportation is documented as observable behavior. The extract does not promote that behavior into an unsupported psychological diagnosis.
- The Scroll of Meat Hooks causes Mongo to abandon training and charge toward its influence; exact radius, duration, and saving mechanics remain unknown.
- Research also contains broader claims about intelligence thresholds, enhanced pet biscuits, and pet-bond transfer restrictions. These are retained as research evidence but should not be treated as authoritative universal mechanics without stronger primary or corroborating evidence.

## Modeling boundary

This artifact is a research extract, not authoritative CCI runtime data. It does not choose event types, runtime payloads, capabilities, or promotion dispositions. Modeling decisions remain a separate review step, and authoritative Floor 3 data remains under `data/raw/floors/floor-3/**`.

The extract intentionally preserves uncertainty and avoids turning specific examples into universal System rules.

## Provenance note

The current evidence set is predominantly secondary. Confidence therefore describes the strength of the evidence for an extracted proposition, not primary-source authority and not CCI promotion status.
